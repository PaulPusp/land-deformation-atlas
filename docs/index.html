    FAQ

    Do I need a prepared file?

    The processing service can build a grid from a selected Sentinel-1 ARIA GUNW product once connected. NISAR search is available, and automatic processing still requires validation.

    What does displacement measure?

    Colors show motion along the satellite line of sight relative to a reference pixel. Positive values indicate motion toward the sensor. A 3D view requires a same-grid elevation layer.

    Why are there missing pixels?

    Low coherence, invalid data, or a different unwrapping component can mask a pixel. Archive footprints establish candidate coverage.
